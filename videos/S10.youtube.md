## Title

Why simulate, and what tools exist — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 10

## Description

Why the program simulates before it flies, the two common tools, and how to recognize the CG and CP markers and the stability number in OpenRocket.

Segment 10 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: The sim says the CG is here. My real rocket balances two inches further back. Which do I believe, and what do I fix?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.
