## Title

Stability margin and the swing test — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 5

## Description

The stability rule, CG ahead of CP by one to three calibers, what happens when it is broken either way, and how to run a swing test.

Segment 5 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: This rocket has its CG one diameter ahead of its CP. I load a heavier motor. More stable or less?
