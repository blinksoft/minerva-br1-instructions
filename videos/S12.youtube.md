## Title

Stability with and without a motor — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 12

## Description

Add a motor to the OpenRocket model, see what it does to CG and stability, run a simulation, and read apogee, rail-exit speed and the optimum delay.

Segment 12 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: On the pad with the motor loaded, is this rocket more stable or less stable than it will be at burnout? Why?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.

BR-1 geometry, masses and flights: the program's OpenRocket file, BR-1.ork (design by Michael Bright)
Motor masses: thrustcurve.org (public domain motor data files)
Screen recording: OpenRocket 24.12, recorded by the program
