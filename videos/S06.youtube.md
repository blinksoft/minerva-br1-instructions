## Title

How a motor is measured — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 6

## Description

What a Newton-second is in plain words, and how to read the motor letter ladder from A to O.

Segment 6 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: An F motor and a G motor: how many times more total push does the G have, at most?
