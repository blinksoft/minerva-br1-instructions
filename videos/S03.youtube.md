## Title

Center of gravity — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 3

## Description

Find the center of gravity of any rocket with a finger or a loop of string, and predict which way it moves when weight is added.

Segment 3 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: I am about to slide a motor into the tail. Which way will the balance point move?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.

BR-1 geometry and masses: the program's OpenRocket file, BR-1.ork (design by Michael Bright)
