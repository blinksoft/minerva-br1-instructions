## Title

What is high power rocketry? — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 1

## Description

What makes a rocket high power instead of a model rocket, and which of the two rockets on the table is which.

Segment 1 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: Which of these three motors makes a rocket high power?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
