## Title

Motor sizes, types and safety — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 9

## Description

Name the common motor diameters, tell a black powder motor from a composite one, and repeat the program's motor handling rules.

Segment 9 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: On the high power pads the igniter goes in last, at the pad. At our low power table the RSO wants the igniter and plug in before you walk up. Why the difference?
