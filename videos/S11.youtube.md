## Title

Building a rocket from scratch in OpenRocket — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 11

## Description

How to start a new OpenRocket design, add the parts of a simple rocket in the right order, then open the program's BR-1 file and understand what you are looking at.

Segment 11 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: I added the fins and the red dot jumped toward the tail. Why the red one and not the blue one?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.

Geometry and masses: the program's BR-1.ork (OpenRocket 24.12), via shared/br1-sim.json
Screen recording: OpenRocket 24.12, recorded by the program
