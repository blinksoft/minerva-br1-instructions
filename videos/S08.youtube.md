## Title

Thrust curves and the delay — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 8

## Description

How to read a thrust curve, point to total impulse, peak thrust and burn time on it, and explain what a wrong delay looks like on the field.

Segment 8 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: Same rocket, same G motor, but a 12 second delay instead of 6. Where is the rocket pointing when the chute comes out?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.

Thrust curve data: thrustcurve.org (public domain motor data files)
Flight data: OpenRocket 24.12 simulations of the BR-1 on a G74W-6 and an H135W (files/BR-1.ork)
