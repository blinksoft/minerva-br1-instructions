## Title

Center of pressure — BR-1 Build Day, Segment 4

## Description

What the center of pressure is, which parts of the rocket make it, and why fins at the back move it aft.

Segment 4 of the "While the epoxy cures" series for the Georgia Wing High Power Rocketry Minerva BR-1 build day (Civil Air Patrol). Each segment is 3 to 5 minutes and fills an epoxy cure wait during the build.

Pause card: when the countdown appears, instructors pause, ask the room, then press play for the answer. This segment asks: If I took the fins off this rocket, which way would the CP move?

Assembly guide: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
Curriculum and scripts: https://github.com/blinksoft/minerva-br1-instructions/tree/main/curriculum

Narration is synthesized (ElevenLabs) from the program's script. Slides are generated from the curriculum source in the guide's repository.

BR-1 geometry and CP: files/BR-1.ork (OpenRocket 24.12) via shared/br1-sim.json

Read more on the Barrowman equations:
James Barrowman, "The Theoretical Prediction of the Center of Pressure", NARAM-8 research report, 1966 (the original; search the title)
Wikipedia, Center of pressure (fluid mechanics): https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Center_of_pressure_(fluid_mechanics)
OpenRocket technical documentation, how the program computes CP and stability: https://openrocket.info/documentation.html
