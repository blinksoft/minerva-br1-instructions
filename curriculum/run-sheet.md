# Build-day run sheet

One page for whoever is leading the build. Print it. Every teaching moment is a video; your job at each
one is to press play, pause when the card says to, take a few answers, press play again.

Videos are in the "BR-1 Build Day" playlist on the program's YouTube channel (IDs in production.md).
Each one is also a numbered step in the assembly guide, at the cure wait it fills, so a cadet following
on a phone sees it at the right moment. Have the playlist open and the room's screen or projector
working before 8:30.

## Before the class

- [ ] Assembly guide open on the screen: https://blinksoft.github.io/minerva-br1-instructions/
- [ ] Playlist open, sound checked at the back of the room
- [ ] Every cadet has the QR code at the top of the guide scanned on their own phone
- [ ] Safety brief from the guide's "Before you start" section read aloud, gloves and glasses on

## At every pause card

1. Pause the video when the card appears. It holds for five seconds and chimes, so you have time.
2. Ask the question on the card. Take two or three answers. Do not give the answer yourself.
3. Press play. The video answers it.

## If you are running late

Skip in this order: S7, S9, S2, S1. Never skip S3, S4, S5 (stability) or S12 (the homework lead-in).
Anything skipped can be sent home as a link with the homework.

## If a video will not play

Each segment file in curriculum/segments/ has the script outline and the pause question. Read the beats
aloud, ask the question, give the answer from the "Pause point" section. It is not as good, but it is
the same material.
