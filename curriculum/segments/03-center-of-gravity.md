# S3 — Center of gravity

## Slot
Step 9 wait (forward ring curing), 5 minutes. Fallback: step 12 wait.

## Objective
The cadet can find the center of gravity of any rocket with a finger or a loop of string and predict which
way it moves when weight is added.

## Live on the day
Play the video. When the pause card comes up, pause it, take two or three answers from the room, then
press play. The video gives the answer. That is the whole job; no props, no setup.

## Script outline
1. **Every object has a balance point.** Balance a pencil on a finger. That point is the center of
   gravity, CG. All the weight acts as if it were there. A rocket in the air spins around its CG.
2. **Find it on a rocket.** Balance the finished Alpha III across a finger. Mark the point with tape. Now
   hang it from a loop of string and slide the loop until it hangs level. Same point. The string method
   is what you will use on the BR-1 because it is too long for a finger.
   - **Pause card.** On screen: "I am about to slide a motor into the tail. Which way will the balance point move?" Hold five seconds with a countdown, then a chime. On resume: Toward the tail. Weight goes in, the CG moves toward it. The video shows it on camera right after the card.
3. **Move it.** Slide a motor into the Alpha III and balance again. The CG moved toward the motor. Tape a
   coin to the nose and it moves forward. Weight goes in, the CG moves toward it. That is the whole rule.
4. **Why we care.** Where the CG is relative to one other point on the rocket decides whether it flies
   straight or tumbles. That other point is next (S4). For now: CG is real, you can find it in ten
   seconds, and it changes every time you change the rocket.
5. **On your BR-1.** Before it ever flies, you will find its CG with a motor loaded and compare it to the
   number OpenRocket predicts (S12). If they disagree, one of them is wrong and it is usually the sim's
   guess at the mass.

## Demo, filmed
- A pencil.
- A finished Alpha III, a motor for it (spent casing is fine on the build table), a loop of string, a coin
  and tape.
- A finished BR-1 to show the string method at full length.

## Pause point
After beat 2. The card reads: "I am about to slide a motor into the tail. Which way will the balance point move?"

On resume the video answers: Toward the tail. Weight goes in, the CG moves toward it. The video shows it on camera right after the card.

## Shot list
- Bench camera, tight on hands: pencil balance, then Alpha III balance, tape mark.
- Motor in, re-balance, second tape mark next to the first so the shift is visible.
- Wide shot: BR-1 hanging level from a string loop.

- Pause card and answer card as full-screen title cards, see production.md.

## Check before filming
- Nothing regulatory. Make sure the demo rocket actually balances cleanly on camera; a smooth tube on a
  fingertip rolls off.
